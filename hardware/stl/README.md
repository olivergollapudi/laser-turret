# Printable parts

The turret is a remix of **Cheap FPV Gimbal – Pan/Tilt by Steve Eckerlein** on Printables. The base, servo mounts and gears are his design, unchanged. Download those from his model page (or from [this project's Printables page](https://www.printables.com/model/1731488-person-tracking-laser-turret-raspberry-pi-python)) and follow his assembly guide.

## New parts in this remix

Put these files in this folder:

| File | What it is | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| `Camera and Laser Mount Holder.stl` / `.step` | New gimbal top part. Replaces the original FPV camera mount and holds the payload mount. | 1 |
| `Camera and Laser Mount.stl` / `.step` | Holds the Pi Camera Module 3 and the 5 mm laser side by side | 1 |

## From the original gimbal

Inner bearing · Camera gear · Spur gear (48 teeth) · Top part 2 · Pan gear · Cross (low profile) · Base (low profile, or the tower base)

## Print settings

PLA · 0.2 mm layers · 3–4 walls · 15–20% infill · supports on.
